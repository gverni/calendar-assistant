Calendar Assistant

Scan lists meetings in your default calendar (with at least one guest email). A debrief is a 15-minute block starting exactly when the meeting ends. Scheduling checks that window against accepted meetings on your calendar only — tentative or unanswered invites don't block it. If it still conflicts, use Schedule anyway. New debriefs try to protect that time (including declining new overlapping invites when your account supports it).
